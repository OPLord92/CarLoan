Car Loan Manager Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Car Loan Manager app handles information when you create an account and use it to track car and motorcycle loan repayments.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, Firebase user identifier, and authentication provider information needed to sign you in.
  • Loan information you enter: vehicle type (car or motorcycle), plate number, total loan amount, and each payment's amount, date and note.
  • Authentication information: when you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, those providers and Firebase Authentication process the information needed to authenticate your account.
  • Display preference: your light/dark theme choice is saved with your account so it matches across your devices.

The app does not store your password on your device. If you tick “Keep me signed in”, your email address is remembered on the device and the sign-in session is kept by Firebase Authentication.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to provide account access, synchronize your Car Loan Manager data across your supported devices, show your loan progress, and operate the features you request. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your loan or payment information for advertising.

Optional Features

  • Export data: when you choose Export data (CSV), a file with your vehicles and payments is created on your device and handed to the app you choose. We do not receive a copy.
  • Monthly payment reminder: if you turn this on, the reminder is scheduled on your device as a local notification. No information is sent to us or to any third party for this feature.
  • Language, currency and reminder settings: these preferences are stored on your device only.

Service Providers

Car Loan Manager uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide sign-in and cloud data storage. If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, the selected provider is used to authenticate you. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Data transmitted between Car Loan Manager and Firebase is sent using encrypted network connections. Access to account data is tied to the authenticated Firebase user account. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is retained while your Car Loan Manager account remains active. You can delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account). You can also use the external account-deletion page below. The deletion process removes your Firebase Authentication account and the Firestore document associated with that account, including saved vehicles and payment records.

Delete your Car Loan Manager account

Children

Car Loan Manager is not designed specifically for children. If the app is later offered to children, the developer will update the app and this policy as required by applicable law and Google Play policies.

Privacy Questions

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the Car Loan Manager Google Play listing. This policy applies specifically to the Car Loan Manager app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Car Loan Manager features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

车贷管家隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月2日

本隐私政策说明您创建账户并使用车贷管家记录汽车和摩托车贷款还款时，我们如何处理相关信息。

我们收集的信息

  • 账户信息：您的电子邮件地址、Firebase 用户识别码，以及登录所需的身份验证服务提供商信息。
  • 您输入的贷款信息：车辆类型（汽车或摩托车）、车牌号码、贷款总额，以及每笔还款的金额、日期和备注。
  • 身份验证信息：当您选择使用 Google 或 Facebook 登录时，相应服务提供商与 Firebase Authentication 会处理完成账户验证所需的信息。
  • 显示偏好：您选择的浅色/深色主题会保存在您的账户中，以便在各个设备上保持一致。

本应用不会在您的设备上保存您的密码。如果您勾选“保持登录”，您的电子邮件地址会记在设备上，登录状态则由 Firebase Authentication 保持。

我们如何使用信息

我们使用上述信息来提供账户访问、在您支持的设备之间同步车贷管家数据、显示贷款进度，以及提供您所使用的功能。我们不会出售您的信息，也不会将您的贷款或还款信息用于广告。

可选功能

  • 导出数据：当您选择导出 CSV 数据时，系统会在您的设备上建立包含车辆和还款记录的文件，并交由您选择的应用程序处理。我们不会收到该文件的副本。
  • 每月还款提醒：若您启用此功能，提醒会以本机通知方式安排在您的设备上。此功能不会将相关信息发送给我们或任何第三方。
  • 语言、货币及提醒设置：这些偏好设置只储存在您的设备上。

服务提供商

车贷管家使用 Google Firebase 服务，包括 Firebase Authentication 与 Cloud Firestore，以提供登录及云端数据储存功能。若您选择 Google 或 Facebook 登录，所选服务提供商会用于验证您的身份，并依据其自身的条款与隐私政策处理相关信息。

数据安全

车贷管家与 Firebase 之间传输的数据会通过加密网络连接发送。账户数据的访问权限与已验证的 Firebase 用户账户绑定。任何电子储存或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

数据保留与删除

只要您的车贷管家账户保持有效，相关账户数据就会被保留。您可以在应用内删除账户（设置 → 删除账户），也可以使用下方的外部账户删除页面。删除流程会移除您的 Firebase Authentication 账户，以及与该账户相关的 Firestore 文档，包括已保存的车辆和还款记录。

儿童

车贷管家并非专为儿童设计。如果日后向儿童提供本应用，开发者将根据适用法律及 Google Play 政策更新应用与本政策。

隐私相关问题

如有隐私相关问题，请使用车贷管家 Google Play 商店页面所列的开发者联系资料。本政策仅适用于车贷管家应用。

本政策的变更

当车贷管家的功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。若有重大变更，页面顶部的生效日期也会相应更新。
